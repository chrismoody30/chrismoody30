I hold a Bachelor of Innovation in Computer Security from the University of Colorado Colorado Springs and currently work in IT, where I serve as the sole technical point of contact for a 150+ person organization. Outside of work, I run a hands-on home lab to deepen my offensive and defensive security skills, and I have a genuine passion for understanding how systems work and how to break and defend them.

My journey in computer science has led me to a passion for cybersecurity, and I am now focused on moving into security operations, specifically aiming to join a Security Operations Center (SOC) as a Tier 1 Analyst. I am actively building the skills and certifications to get there.
